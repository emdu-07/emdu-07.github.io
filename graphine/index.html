Motion Graphs & Animation

Graphine

An AI agent that streamlines animation steps and motion curves

I occasionally do graphic design and animation in Alight Motion, and I wanted to make the animation workflow more efficient. I often found it difficult to translate the motion I had in mind into precise easing curves. I was inspired by tools like cubic-bezier.com, which let you adjust a curve and see how the motion changes.

With Graphine, I wanted to approach that problem from the opposite direction: start with the motion itself, then generate the easing curve and animation instructions needed to recreate it.

Start with a shape or import your own image, choose how long to record, and move your object. The motion guide shows how its horizontal position, vertical position, and rotation change over time.

A simple shape is a starting point, but a familiar character makes experimenting more fun. Import an image onto the canvas, then use it as the object for your motion recording.

Choose a recording duration, press record, and move the object after the countdown. As the preview above shows, Graphine translates that movement into separate X-position and Y-position curves, so you can connect what happens on the canvas with what happens on the graph.

Moving sideways changes the horizontal position; moving up and down changes the vertical position. Try different paths and speeds to explore how the curves change, then replay the recorded motion with the timeline controls.

Position tells only part of the story. Rotate an object with its rotation handle to explore how its angle changes over time. The rotation graph adds another dimension to the motion guide, alongside the two position curves.
